Ratb (رطب, "moist") is a calm, beautiful tasbeeh counter and prayer companion. Free, with no ads, no account and no tracking.

TASBEEH COUNTER
• Tap anywhere on the screen to count, with a gentle vibration on every tap
• A bead ring that fills as you go, with a clear "complete" at 33, 99 or any target
• Pocket mode for counting without looking
• Your own dhikr with your own targets

ADHKAR THAT MOVE ON BY THEMSELVES
• After salah, morning, evening and before sleep routines
• Each step moves to the next when you finish, so you never lose your place
• 33 duas for everyday needs, with meaning and source

SALAH TRACKER
• Tick each prayer as you pray it and keep your streak
• See your week and a calendar of every day you prayed
• Qada tracker: set how many prayers you owe and count them down as you make them up

PRAYER TIMES AND REMINDERS
• Accurate prayer times for your location, calculated on your phone
• Reminders after each prayer, with an optional azan
• Your mosque's jamaat times, with a reminder before jamaat
• Share your mosque's timetable with one link
• Qibla compass

QURAN
• All 114 surahs with English or Urdu translation
• Works offline, with bookmarks

WIDGETS
• Prayer times, next prayer, salah tracker and verse of the day on your home screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics
• Your counts, prayers and history stay on your phone

Available in English, العربية and اردو.
