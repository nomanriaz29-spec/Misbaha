DESCRIPTION
Ratb is built around the mosque you actually pray at.

Prayer apps alert you at the azan time. But your mosque prays jamaat at its own time, and in a city like Lahore every mosque is different. Ratb keeps your mosque's jamaat times, shows the next jamaat with a countdown on the main screen, and reminds you before jamaat so you reach the first takbir.

YOUR MOSQUE
• Find nearby mosques on the map, or type your mosque's name
• Set each jamaat as minutes after the azan, or as a fixed time, plus Jumu'ah
• Next jamaat and countdown on the main screen
• A reminder 10 to 30 minutes before jamaat

SHARE THE TIMETABLE
• One link carries your mosque's full jamaat timetable
• Post it in the family or mosque WhatsApp group: anyone can read the times, and Ratb users add them in one tap
• No accounts and no servers: the timetable travels inside the link

TRACK YOUR SALAH, AND MAKE UP WHAT WAS MISSED
• Tick each prayer in the app, from the "Prayed" button on the notification, or on the widget
• Streak of days with all five, and your week at a glance
• Qada tracker: estimate prayers missed from years and months, or enter each prayer, then tick them off as you make them up

TASBEEH
• Tap anywhere to count, with a gentle vibration on each count
• Routines after each prayer, morning and evening adhkar, before sleep
• Pocket mode for counting without looking
• Ratb on Apple Watch: count with a tap or the Digital Crown

ALSO INCLUDED
• Prayer times calculated on your phone for where you are, with the azan
• Full Quran with English (Pickthall) or Urdu (Jalandhry) translation, offline
• Home and Lock Screen widgets: prayer times, jamaat, a daily verse, the salah tracker
• Hijri calendar, Ramadan suhoor and iftar countdown, Qibla compass
• Fully in English, Arabic and Urdu, including right-to-left layouts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your prayers, counts and mosque stay on your phone.

APP REVIEW NOTES
What's changed since the last review (guideline 4.3):
Ratb is now organised around the user's own mosque, which generic prayer and tasbeeh apps don't do:
1. Jamaat on the main screen: with a mosque set, the counter shows the next jamaat at that mosque with a countdown.
2. Shareable mosque timetables: My mosque > Share this timetable sends a readable message with a link. The link opens a timetable page that anyone can read, and "Open in Ratb" imports it. Users can also paste a received message under "Got a timetable from someone?". There is no server; the timetable is encoded in the link.
3. Qada tracker: History > Qada prayers. Estimate prayers owed from years and months missed, or enter each prayer, then tap +1 as each is made up.
4. Apple Watch app: a standalone tasbeeh counter (tap or Digital Crown).
5. First launch now asks "Do you pray at a mosque?" and opens My mosque.

How to try it:
- Allow location (or pick a city), then choose "Choose my mosque". Tap "Find mosques near me", or type any name, e.g. "Test Mosque".
- The jamaat card then appears on the main screen. In My mosque, tap "Share this timetable".
- To test importing, open this link on the device: https://nomanriaz29-spec.github.io/Misbaha/m/#RATB1.eyJuIjoiSmFtaWEgTWFzamlkIEFsLU5vb3IiLCJqIjpbImEyMCIsImEzMCIsImEyMCIsImE1IiwiZjE5OjMwIiwiZjEzOjMwIl19 then tap "Open in Ratb".
- Prayer tracking: tap a prayer in Prayers or History, or use "Prayed" on a prayer notification (Prayers > Send a test notification).

No sign-in is needed. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or in-app purchases. The only network request is the optional mosque search (OpenStreetMap Overpass), sent with the location rounded to about 1 km.
