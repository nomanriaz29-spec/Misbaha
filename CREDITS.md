# Credits

## Azan recording

`assets/sounds/azan.wav` (the 17-second opening used for notifications) and `assets/sounds/azan-full.m4a` (the complete azan played in the app) are made from **"Beautiful adhan.ogg"** on Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg

It is listed as **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** by the open-source projects that bundle it ([qibla-mobile](https://github.com/rahmanSaaed/qibla-mobile) and [islami_app](https://github.com/AhmedAhmedEzzat/islami_app/pull/5)). **Before publishing, open the Commons page and confirm the licence shown there.** If it isn't public domain, replace both files with a recording you have permission to use, keeping the same file names. The notification clip must stay under 30 seconds.

Changes made: trimmed to the first "Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar" with a short fade (notification clip), loudness-normalised, converted to mono WAV / AAC.
