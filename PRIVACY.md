# Ratb Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Ratb is a tasbeeh counter with prayer-time reminders, duas and a Qibla finder. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers.

## Location
With your permission, Ratb reads your phone's approximate location once, during setup or when you tap "Use my location". It uses it to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction on your phone, with no server involved. The location is rounded to about 1 km and stored only on your phone.

Your phone works out its location with its own location service. Besides GPS, this service may check nearby Wi-Fi networks and cell towers with Apple (on iPhone) or Google (on Android) to find your position faster. This is part of your phone's operating system, it follows Apple's or Google's privacy policy, and you control it in your phone's location settings.

To show your city name, Ratb asks your phone's built-in mapping service to turn the coordinates into a place name. That service is Apple on iPhone and Google on Android, and they handle the request under their own privacy policies.

If you tap "Find mosques near me" (Prayers → My mosque), Ratb sends your location, rounded to about 1 km, to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service to list the mosques around you. This happens only when you tap that button, and the request carries no name, account or device identifier. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. The mosque you pick and its jamaat times are stored only on your phone.

Ratb does not send your location anywhere else.

Ratb never uses your location in the background. You can turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time and choose a city instead.

## Compass
The Qibla finder reads your phone's compass heading only while that screen is open. The heading is not stored.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

## Your counts and history
Your tasbeeh counts, prayer tracker, daily history and settings are stored only on your phone. If your phone backs up apps (iCloud or Google backup), this data may be included in your own backup. Deleting the app deletes the data from your phone.

## What we collect
Nothing. Ratb does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not track you.

## Children
Ratb is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
