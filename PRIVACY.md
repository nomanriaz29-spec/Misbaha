# Misbaha Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Misbaha is a tasbeeh counter with prayer-time reminders.

**Location.** With your permission, Misbaha reads your device's location to calculate prayer times. Your location stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party. You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings and choose a city by hand instead.

**Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server is involved.

**Counts and settings.** Your tasbeeh counts and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

**No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** Misbaha does not collect, sell or share personal data.
