# Misbaha Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Misbaha is a tasbeeh counter with prayer-time reminders, duas and a Qibla finder. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers.

## Location
With your permission, Misbaha reads your phone's approximate location while the app is open. It uses it to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The location is rounded to about 1 km and stored only on your phone.

To show your city name, Misbaha asks your phone's built-in mapping service to turn the coordinates into a place name. That service is Apple on iPhone and Google on Android, and they handle the request under their own privacy policies. Misbaha does not send your location anywhere else.

Misbaha never uses your location in the background. You can turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time and choose a city instead.

## Compass
The Qibla finder reads your phone's compass heading only while that screen is open. The heading is not stored.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

## Your counts and history
Your tasbeeh counts, daily history and settings are stored only on your phone. If your phone backs up apps (iCloud or Google backup), this data may be included in your own backup. Deleting the app deletes the data from your phone.

## What we collect
Nothing. Misbaha does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not track you.

## Children
Misbaha is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
